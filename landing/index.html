Questions

The things people ask

Is my pet's data private?

Completely. Pawday stores everything locally on your device. There is no account, no login, no server and no analytics on your pet's health records. If you delete the app the data goes with it, which is exactly why Pro includes a full backup export.

Does the free version actually work, or is it a demo?

It genuinely works. One pet, six daily reminders, real notifications, streaks, quests, awards, a twenty-photo scrapbook and weight tracking — free forever. We gate depth and scale, never care itself.

Should I pick the subscription or Lifetime?

If you want to decide once and never think about it again, Lifetime is the better deal after about two and a half years — and pets are a decade-long commitment. If you would rather try it properly first, the yearly plan starts with seven free days and costs less than a bag of treats a month.

Which pets does it support?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, small pets, reptiles and fish each get their own suggested starter routine — hay top-ups for rabbits, water tests for fish, heat and humidity checks for reptiles. Anything else fits under "Other" with a routine you write yourself.

Will it nag me?

No. Reminders fire at the times you set, plus at most one gentle nudge in the evening if something is still open. Miss a day and Pawday says "streaks end, care doesn't" — never guilt, never a storm of red badges.

Is this a substitute for a vet?

Absolutely not, and it never pretends to be. Pawday keeps records and reminders. Diagnosis and treatment are your vet's job — the app just makes sure you turn up to the appointment with the facts.
